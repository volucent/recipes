# Mediterranean Chicken with Sumac Salad

Adapted from a thigh recipe — swapped to **chicken breast**. Yields ~4 servings. Cast iron + cutting board + blender (optional). Salad and sauce are no-cook.

## Ingredients

### Chicken Marinade
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1.5 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp dried thyme
- ½ tbsp red chili flakes
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 tbsp plain yogurt
- Juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and black pepper

### Sumac Salad
- 1 large red onion, thinly sliced
- Small handful cherry tomatoes, halved
- Large handful flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp sumac
- Juice of 1 large lemon
- 2-3 pinches salt

### Dill Yogurt Sauce
- ¼ cup plain yogurt
- 3 tbsp mayo
- ⅛ to ¼ cup pickle juice
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- Pinch of salt
- Lots of fresh cracked pepper

### To Serve
- Cooked rice (regular, basmati, or low-carb)
- Sliced Persian cucumbers
- Fresh herbs

## Method

### 1. Marinate the chicken (5 min + 4-6 hr ideal)
Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Add chicken. Mix well. Marinate 4-6 hr in fridge if possible, or skip ahead if short on time.

**Breast tip:** if breasts are thick (>1 inch), butterfly them or pound to even thickness. Breasts dry out faster than thighs — even thickness is key.

### 2. Cook the chicken (10-12 min)
Bring chicken to room temp 20 min before cooking.

- Heat cast iron pan over medium with 1 tbsp oil.
- Cook breasts 5-6 min per side, depending on thickness.
- Internal temp must hit 165°F.
- **Critical:** rest 5 min before slicing or the juices will run out.

### 3. Sumac salad (10 min)
Combine onion, tomatoes, parsley in a bowl. Add sumac, lemon juice, and salt. Stir. Let sit 10 min, stirring once or twice. The onion softens and goes pink.

### 4. Dill yogurt sauce (2 min)
Whisk all sauce ingredients together. Taste and adjust salt/pickle juice. Should be tangy.

## Assembly
- Rice base in a bowl.
- Sliced chicken on top.
- Pile sumac salad high.
- Sliced cucumbers and fresh herbs around the edge.
- Drizzle dill yogurt sauce over everything.

## Bulk Prep Notes
- Marinate and cook 2 lb chicken in one go. Stores 4 days in fridge.
- Sauce holds 5 days.
- Salad is best fresh (onion gets too soft after a day). Slice and store onion/parsley/tomatoes separately, dress when serving.
- Reheat chicken gently — microwave with damp paper towel over the top, or low heat in a covered pan with a splash of water.

## Instant Pot Variation
If skipping the sear:
- Add ¼ cup water or broth to the pot.
- Add marinated chicken on top.
- HIGH pressure 8 min, natural release 5 min.
- Loses the crust but gains a hands-off bulk option.
