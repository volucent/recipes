# Harissa Lime Chicken with Pineapple Salsa

Bulk meal prep. ~3.5 lb chicken yields ~6-8 servings. Eat as bowls (over rice) or in tortillas.

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 3.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 3 tbsp harissa
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- Zest of 2 limes
- Juice of 2 limes
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper

### Pineapple Salsa
- 3 cups pineapple, diced small
- 2 tsp honey
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- Pinch of salt

### Herb Yogurt Sauce
- 1½ cups nonfat Greek yogurt
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### To Serve
- 12 large low-carb tortillas
- Optional: 2 cups long grain white or basmati rice

## Method

### 1. Marinate the chicken (10 min + ideally 1-4 hr rest)
Combine harissa, olive oil, paprika, cumin, garlic powder, lime zest, lime juice, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Add the chicken, coat thoroughly. Marinate at least 30 min, ideally 1-4 hr in the fridge.

### 2. Cook the chicken — pick one

**Instant Pot (hands-off, best for bulk):**
- Add 1/2 cup water or chicken broth to the pot.
- Place chicken with marinade on top (do not stir into liquid).
- Pressure cook on HIGH for 10 min, then natural release 5 min.
- Internal temp should be 165°F. Shred or slice.

**Oven (best texture, easy bulk):**
- Preheat oven to 425°F.
- Spread chicken on a sheet pan in a single layer.
- Roast 18-22 min until internal temp hits 165°F.
- Rest 5 min, then slice.

**Cast iron pan (best sear, smaller batches):**
- Heat pan over medium-high with 1 tbsp oil.
- Sear chicken 4-5 min per side. Internal temp 165°F.
- Rest 5 min, slice.

### 3. Pineapple salsa (5 min)
Toss diced pineapple with honey, red pepper flakes, and salt. Let sit 10 min so juices release.

### 4. Herb yogurt sauce (2 min)
Whisk yogurt, lemon juice, cilantro, minced garlic, and salt in a bowl. Refrigerate until serving.

### 5. Optional rice
Cook 2 cups rice in rice cooker or Instant Pot (1:1.25 rice:water for basmati, 12 min high pressure + 10 min natural release).

## Assembly
- **Bowl:** rice base, sliced chicken, pineapple salsa, drizzle of yogurt sauce, fresh cilantro.
- **Tortilla:** warm tortilla, chicken, salsa, yogurt sauce.

## Bulk Prep Notes
- Cook the full 3.5 lb at once.
- Store chicken, salsa, and sauce in separate containers (4-day fridge life).
- Reheat chicken with a splash of water to keep moist.
- Salsa is best fresh — make day-of if possible, but it holds 2 days.
