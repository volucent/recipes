# Chocolate Cottage Cheese Protein Ice Cream

Ninja Creami pint. A version of the [chocolate PB recipe](chocolate-peanut-butter-protein.md) with no cream and no PB powder. Blended cottage cheese stands in for the cream. Untested — adjust after the first batch.

## Ingredients

- 1¼ cups milk
- ¾ cup cottage cheese (or Greek yogurt — tangier, more like frozen yogurt)
- 1 scoop Thorne chocolate protein
- ½ scoop Thorne vanilla protein
- 1-2 tbsp cocoa powder
- Splash of vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- ⅛ tsp xanthan gum
- Sweetener to taste (honey, maple syrup, or any sugar-free sweetener)

## Method

### 1. Blend (2 min)
Use a blender, not a shaker bottle — the cottage cheese has to be completely smooth or you'll get curds in the ice cream. Blend everything except the xanthan gum, then add it while the blender runs.

**Taste before freezing.** Regular milk isn't sweet like Fairlife chocolate milk, and cocoa is bitter. The mix should taste a little sweeter than you want, because sweetness fades when frozen.

### 2. Freeze (24 hr)
Pour into a Creami pint up to the max fill line. Lid on, freeze flat for 24 hours.

### 3. Spin
- Let the pint sit out 5-10 min before spinning.
- Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**.
- Regular milk freezes harder than Fairlife, so expect to **Re-spin** once or twice with 1-2 tbsp milk.

## Notes
- All chocolate: use 1½ scoops Thorne chocolate and skip the vanilla scoop.
- Sweetener options: 1-2 tbsp honey or maple syrup also makes it softer, because sugar keeps it from freezing rock-hard. A sugar-free sweetener keeps calories down but gives a harder pint.
