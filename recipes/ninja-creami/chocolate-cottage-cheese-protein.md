# Chocolate Cottage Cheese Protein Ice Cream

Ninja Creami XL, one 24 oz pint. A version of the [chocolate PB recipe](chocolate-peanut-butter-protein.md) with no cream and no PB powder. Blended cottage cheese stands in for the cream. See [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) for machine rules.

Untested. The source video's creator says cottage cheese bases taste bad. If it doesn't work, swap the cottage cheese for Greek yogurt or more milk.

- **Setting:** Lite Ice Cream (not yet tested — also try Ice Cream and keep the winner)
- **Freeze:** 24 hr, lid off
- **Spin:** Lite Ice Cream → Re-spin if powdery → Mix-In if 90–95% smooth

## Ingredients

- 460 g (1⅞ cups) milk (1% or higher; high-protein milk like Fairlife is smoother)
- 255 g (1⅛ cups) full-fat cottage cheese (or Greek yogurt — tangier, more like frozen yogurt)
- 1½ scoops Thorne chocolate protein (weigh per the label's serving size)
- ¾ scoop Thorne vanilla protein
- 8–15 g (1½–3 tbsp) cocoa powder
- ~3 g (½ tsp) vanilla extract
- ~0.5 g salt (a good pinch)
- ~0.75 g xanthan gum
- Sweetener to taste: erythritol or other sugar-free sweetener, plus ~15 g sugar or honey to cut the aftertaste

## Method

### 1. Blend (3 min)
Weigh everything except the xanthan gum into the pint. Blend with an immersion blender until the cottage cheese is completely smooth (no curds), then add the xanthan gum while blending. Keep going ~90 s after it looks smooth.

**Taste before freezing.** Regular milk isn't sweet like Fairlife chocolate milk, and cocoa is bitter. Make it a little sweeter than you want, since sweetness fades when frozen.

### 2. Rest and freeze (24 hr)
Check the liquid is at or below the **MAX FILL** line (foam above it is fine). Let it sit 15 min so the foam rises and settles. Freeze flat, **lid off**, for 24 hours.

### 3. Spin
- Run hot water over the outside of the pint for 1 min.
- Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**.
- Regular milk freezes harder than Fairlife, so expect to add 2–3 tbsp milk and **Re-spin** once or twice.
- Almost smooth but not quite: one run on **Mix-In** finishes it.

## Notes
- All chocolate: use 2¼ scoops Thorne chocolate and skip the vanilla.
- Real sugar or honey also makes the pint softer. An all-sugar-free version will spin harder.
