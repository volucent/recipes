# Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Ice Cream

Ninja Creami XL, one 24 oz pint. High protein, low sugar. See [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) for machine rules.

- **Setting:** Lite Ice Cream (not yet tested — also try Ice Cream and keep the winner)
- **Freeze:** 24 hr, lid off
- **Spin:** Lite Ice Cream → Re-spin if powdery → Mix-In if 90–95% smooth

## Ingredients

- 640 g (2⅔ cups) Fairlife chocolate milk
- 90 g (6 tbsp) heavy whipping cream (keeps it creamy; if skipping, use 730 g / 3 cups milk)
- 2¼ scoops vanilla protein powder (the Costco one — weigh per the label's serving size; brand really affects taste)
- 1½ scoops peanut butter powder
- 15–20 g (3–4 tbsp) cocoa powder
- ~3 g (½ tsp) vanilla extract
- ~0.5 g salt (a good pinch)
- ~0.75 g xanthan gum
- Sugar-free sweetener to taste (TODO: add the one from the video)

## Method

### 1. Blend (3 min)
Weigh everything except the xanthan gum into the pint. Blend with an immersion blender, then add the xanthan gum while blending. Keep going ~90 s after it looks dissolved. Taste — it should be a little sweeter than you want, since sweetness fades when frozen.

### 2. Rest and freeze (24 hr)
Check the liquid is at or below the **MAX FILL** line (foam above it is fine). Let it sit 15 min so the foam rises and settles. Freeze flat, **lid off**, for 24 hours.

### 3. Spin
- Run hot water over the outside of the pint for 1 min.
- Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**.
- Powdery or crumbly: add 2–3 tbsp milk and **Re-spin**.
- Almost smooth but not quite: one run on **Mix-In** finishes it.

## Notes
- Protein powder makes or breaks the flavor — swap brands and the result changes a lot. If the Costco powder is a whey–casein blend, it can over-thicken; cut back if the texture is dense.
- Mix-ins (chopped peanut butter cups): freeze them first, make the hole off-center, add them fast, and run **Mix-In** after the final spin.
