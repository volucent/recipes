# Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Ice Cream

Ninja Creami pint. High protein, low sugar. Fills one pint to the max fill line.

## Ingredients

- 1¾ cups Fairlife chocolate milk
- ¼ cup heavy whipping cream (optional — if skipping, use 2 cups milk total)
- 1½ scoops vanilla protein powder (the Costco one — brand really affects taste)
- 1 scoop peanut butter powder
- 2-3 spoonfuls cocoa powder
- Small splash of vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Pinch of xanthan gum (about ⅛-¼ tsp)
- Sugar-free sweetener to taste (TODO: add the one from the video)

## Method

### 1. Blend (2 min)
Add everything to a blender or shake in a shaker bottle until no clumps of powder remain. Add the xanthan gum last while blending so it doesn't clump.

### 2. Freeze (24 hr)
Pour into a Creami pint up to the max fill line. Lid on, freeze flat for 24 hours.

### 3. Spin
- Let the pint sit out 5-10 min (or run under warm water ~1 min) before spinning.
- Spin on **Lite Ice Cream**.
- If it comes out powdery or crumbly, add 1-2 tbsp milk and **Re-spin**. Repeat once more if needed.

## Notes
- Protein powder makes or breaks the flavor — swap brands and the result changes a lot.
- Optional mix-ins: chopped peanut butter cups or a drizzle of PB — use the **Mix-In** setting after the final spin.
